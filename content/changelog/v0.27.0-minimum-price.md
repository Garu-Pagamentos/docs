# Minimum price of R$ 5,00, and two access rules tightened

**Released:** 2026-09-30 (gateway v0.27.0)

Every price a seller sets must now be at least **R$ 5,00**, the platform minimum price. A product can still have no price at all (`value: 0`). Two routes also stopped trusting ids that belong to another seller.

## Minimum price

| Field | Accepted | Refused with `400` |
|---|---|---|
| Product `value` (`POST/PATCH /api/v1/products`, `/api/products`) | `0` (no price), or `5.00` and above | `0.01` to `4.99`, and negatives |
| Offer `value` (`POST /api/v1/products/{uuid}/offers`, `PATCH /api/v1/offers/{offerId}`) | `5.00` and above | `0` to `4.99`, and negatives |
| Subscription price `amount` (create) / `unitAmount` (update) | `5.00` and above | `0` to `4.99`, and negatives |

- The message is `O preço mínimo de um produto é R$ 5,00.`. For a product it continues with `Use R$ 0,00 para um produto sem preço.`.
- The rule applies when a price is **set**: on create, and on an update that sends the price. An update without the price keeps the old one, so products, offers and plans already priced below R$ 5,00 keep selling. Nothing changed at checkout.
- A product with `value: 0` cannot be sold through its payment link: the processor refuses a R$ 0,00 charge. Use it for a product billed another way, such as scheduled charges.
- A subscription product (`isSubscription: true`) is not checked on the product itself. Its price lives on its subscription prices, which are checked. Turning a subscription product into a one-time product counts as setting its price.
- On `POST /api/v1/products`, `value` stays required: send `0` for a product with no price.

## Units

All of these are **reais** (decimal BRL), never centavos: product `value`, offer `value`, subscription price `amount` / `unitAmount`, and scheduled-charge `amount`. The gateway's OpenAPI described the v1 subscription-price `amount` as cents (example `2990`); it has always been stored and charged as reais, and the description now says so.

## Access rules

- **`DELETE /api/transactions/{id}`** (dashboard route, `{id}` is the `galaxPayId`) now cancels only a charge that belongs to the caller's seller. Any other id answers `404 Transação não encontrada`. This route is not part of the public API; integrators cancel with `DELETE /api/v1/charges/{uuid}`, which was already scoped.
- **`POST /api/v1/scheduled-charges/{id}/payment-method`** (and the dashboard twin) now binds only a card that belongs to the series' customer **and** already bills one of the seller's charges for that customer: a subscription, a scheduled charge, or a past attempt. Any other card answers `404 Payment method not found.`. A card of another customer used to answer `400`.

## Docs

- Product create pages list `image` and `value` as required on `/api/v1/products`, and payment methods as off by default, matching the API.
- The subscription-price update page uses `unitAmount` and `trialPeriodDays`, the fields the API reads. The `amount` it showed before was ignored on update.
- `DELETE /api/transactions/{id}` is marked as an internal route and points to `DELETE /api/v1/charges/{uuid}`.
